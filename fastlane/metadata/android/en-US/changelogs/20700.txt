### Release v2.7.0

✨ New Features
- make distracting apps dull and blurry with a screen filter, no computer or root needed
- choose whether the screen filter shades, blurs, or both
- switch grayscale, extra dim and the screen filter on and off separately
- warn when the phone stops DetoxDroid in the background, with an optional keepalive
- exempt DetoxDroid from battery optimization from the home screen
- turn the about screen into about & settings

🐛 Bug Fixes
- hand the system display filters back when DetoxDroid stops, instead of leaving the screen gray and dimmed
- start a feature when its schedule opens, not only stop it when the schedule ends
- stop losing the foreground app between window events, which left effects stuck on or off
